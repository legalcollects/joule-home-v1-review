Our Approach

Disciplined development, from ground to grid.

Every Joule project follows the same sequence: control the right land, secure the power, build the infrastructure, and hold the asset with partners aligned for the long term.

The Method

Five stages. One standard.

Land without power is acreage. Power without land is a queue position.

  1. 01

    Identify Land

    At or near existing substations, with fiber, access, and a welcoming jurisdiction.

  2. 02

    Secure Power

    Utility service agreements executed before a site is offered.

  3. 03

    Entitle & Build Out

    Zoning, permitting, and site infrastructure carried by Joule.

  4. 04

    Develop the Asset

    Powered sites and campuses with room to expand.

  5. 05

    Partner

    Structures aligned with utilities, operators, landowners, and capital.
