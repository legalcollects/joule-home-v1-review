About Joule

A land and infrastructure company built for permanence.

Joule Land Company exists to do one thing well: convert strategic American land into energy-enabled infrastructure that serves industry for generations.

Who We Are

Owners, not intermediaries

We are a development company. We take ownership positions, carry projects through entitlement and infrastructure, and stay accountable to the outcome. Based in San Diego. Developing real estate since 1985.

Track Record

Developing across sectors since 1985

We have developed everything from multifamily to industrial to special-purpose projects, specializing in the entitlements and infrastructure improvements each sector depends on. That includes approvals in highly contested markets and construction on demanding sites, as demand shifts across the United States.

Why Land and Power

The scarce resource is speed to power

Demand for electricity keeps rising. A site that can be energized quickly serves the user who needs it, the utility that supplies it, and the community around it.

Built for the Long Term

Infrastructure rewards patience

We hold through cycles, invest ahead of demand, and measure success in durable, energized infrastructure.
