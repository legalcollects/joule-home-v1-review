Overview

Joule Land Company (“Joule,” “we,” “us”) respects the privacy of everyone who visits this website or corresponds with us. This policy describes what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

  • Information you provide. When you submit an inquiry, we receive the details you choose to share — your name, organization, contact information, and the substance of your message.
  • Technical information. Like most websites, our hosting infrastructure records standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) for security and reliability.

How We Use Information

We use the information you provide solely to respond to your inquiry and conduct the business relationship you initiate. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties except service providers who help us operate (such as email and form-processing services) or where required by law.

Confidential Business Information

Inquiries frequently include sensitive detail about land holdings, power positions, or capital mandates. We treat that information as confidential business correspondence and restrict access to it within the company.

Cookies & Analytics

This site does not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking. If privacy-respecting analytics are enabled in the future, they will be configured without personal identifiers.

Retention & Your Choices

We retain correspondence for as long as it remains relevant to an actual or potential business relationship. To ask what we hold about you, or to request deletion, contact info@joulelandcompany.com.

Changes

We will post any material changes to this policy on this page with an updated effective date.
