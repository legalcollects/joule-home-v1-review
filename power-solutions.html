Power Solutions

Power is the product. Land is how we deliver it.

Joule treats energy as a first-order asset — secured early, engineered deliberately, and scaled to the requirements of AI and industrial loads measured in hundreds of megawatts.

Capabilities

From queue position to energized campus

Six disciplines, coordinated under one owner, so that capacity arrives when the load does.

i

Utility Interconnection

Interconnection strategy, queue management, and study advancement conducted in close coordination with serving utilities and regional transmission organizations.

ii

On-Site Generation

Evaluation and integration of dedicated generation — natural gas, renewables, and storage — where it accelerates delivery or strengthens reliability for the end user.

iii

Grid Strategy

Market-by-market analysis of capacity, congestion, and expansion plans, so each site’s power thesis rests on how the grid actually behaves — not on averages.

iv

Substations

Siting, design coordination, and delivery of on-site and shared substation infrastructure engineered for phased industrial expansion.

v

Transmission

Corridor planning, easement strategy, and coordination with transmission owners to connect deliverable capacity to the campuses that need it.

vi

Scalable Power for AI & Industry

Phased capacity roadmaps that match hyperscale and industrial deployment schedules — initial energization, committed expansion, and long-term headroom.

Why It Matters

The interconnection queue is the new frontier of site selection

Across U.S. markets, requests for new large-load service now routinely take years. For an AI or industrial user, that delay is the difference between a viable site and a stranded one.

Joule’s answer is to carry the power work ahead of the tenant: interconnection positions advanced before marketing, generation options preserved rather than promised, and utility relationships built through consistent, credible dealings. When a counterparty engages a Joule site, the hardest questions have already been asked — and answered.
